Record a shared e-rickshaw ride · ई-रिक्शा सवारी रिकॉर्ड करें

  1. Get on a shared e-rickshaw near a metro station.
  2. Tap Start ride and keep this screen open until you get off.
  3. Tap Pickup / drop whenever the rickshaw stops for passengers.
  4. At the end, add the fare and send the ride.

Your location stays on your phone until you choose to send the ride. Nothing is uploaded automatically.
